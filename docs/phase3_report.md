# Phase 3 Report — Drift Model

The rules were pre-registered in `docs/phase3_preregistration.md` (commit
`dbff2d5`, 02:38 UTC) before any result was computed. The full tables are in
`docs/phase3_results.md` and `data/backtests/phase3_*.csv`. They are
regenerated by `python -m engine drift`.

## Result

**Gate: PASS on the historical proxy.**

The primary strategy (D0) picks the side the market moved toward between the
opening line and the close. It is graded against the frozen opening line, on
the SBRO proxy for 2007–2021:

| Strategy | W | L | Pushes | N | Win rate | SE | z vs 50% | z vs bar (51.37%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D0: sign of drift (700 no-move games excluded) | 1678 | 1382 | 80 | 3060 | **54.84%** | 0.90 pp | 5.38 | **2.85** |
| D0-full: every game, no move → away | 2016 | 1705 | 119 | 3721 | 54.18% | 0.82 pp | 5.12 | — |
| D-k walk-forward (threshold chosen on prior seasons), 2010–2021 | 1606 | 1372 | — | 2978 | 53.93% | 0.91 pp | 4.31 | — |

- Every one of the 15 seasons is above 50%. The range is 51.2% (2018) to
  61.0% (2007).
- The walk-forward threshold picked k = 1 in most seasons, which means
  ignoring moves of half a point.

**Monotonicity: mostly yes.** The edge grows with the size of the move, except
for half-point moves, which carry no signal:

| Move size (pts) | N | Win rate | SE |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 895 | 48.5% | 1.7 pp |
| 1 | 624 | 54.0% | 2.0 pp |
| 1.5 | 444 | 54.7% | 2.4 pp |
| 2–2.5 | 597 | 61.1% | 2.0 pp |
| 3–4 | 350 | 59.1% | 2.6 pp |
| ≥ 4.5 | 150 | 61.3% | 4.0 pp |

A logistic regression of the chance the picked side covers on the size of the
move gives a slope of 0.128 per point (SE 0.027).

**Key numbers.** Moves that crossed 3 or 7 won 57.5% (N = 1452). Moves that
did not cross won 52.4% (N = 1608).

**Robustness checks** (diagnostic):

| Check | N | Win rate |
|---|---|---|
| Drop moves ≥ 4.5 points (some look like SBRO opening-line errors) | 2910 | 54.5% |
| Use the nflverse close instead of the SBRO close | 3023 | 55.6% |

## What this does and does not show

It shows that, historically, the market's movement after the open predicted
which side would cover the frozen opening number. This matches the core
thesis in the spec.

It does **not** yet show the edge against *your* league line. There are three
gaps, stated in advance in the pre-registration:

1. **Timing of the frozen line.** SBRO's open is the early-week opener. CBS
   posts the league line around noon ET Tuesday, after the first wave of
   movement, and the league line already reflects that wave. The real drift
   (market at lock − CBS Tuesday line) will be smaller than SBRO
   close − open, so the real edge is likely **smaller than 54.8%**. By how
   much is unknown.
2. **Close vs lock.** The close includes the last 15 minutes before kickoff,
   which a pick locked at kickoff − 15 cannot see.
3. **Source book.** Both SBRO lines come from an unnamed book, and the
   league line is set by CBS.

Measuring all three needs 2026 data, which is now being collected:
- the league line (your Tuesday screenshots),
- the market around posting time (the Tuesday capture window),
- the market near lock (the game-day capture window).

After a few weeks, drift can be computed exactly as the league experiences it.

## Operational consequence

The signal is **the market line at lock**. GitHub's scheduled runs start hours
late (audit 0.10), so a snapshot at kickoff − 20 min cannot be guaranteed that
way. **The scheduler decision is now blocking** for Phase 3 to be used live.
The options are the ones in audit 0.10:
- a local or always-on machine, or
- an external trigger calling `workflow_dispatch`.
