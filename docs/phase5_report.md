# Phase 5 Report — Calibration

The rules were pre-registered in `docs/phase5_preregistration.md` (commit
`bbb11b7`) before any calibration was run. The full tables are in
`docs/phase5_results.md` and `data/backtests/phase5_*.csv`. They are
regenerated by `python -m engine calibrate`.

## What was tested

Three calibrators were compared with the raw Phase 4 probabilities:
- Platt scaling,
- isotonic regression,
- Platt with a key-number term.

Each was fitted **only on earlier seasons'** out-of-sample predictions and
then applied to the next season. Test seasons are 2010–2021, 2,979 games.

## Result: no calibrator qualified, so the engine stays **raw**

| Method | Log loss | Brier | ECE | ATS of P > 0.5 | Picks changed vs raw |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **raw** | **0.6875** | **0.2472** | 0.0284 | 54.45% ± 0.91 pp | — |
| platt | 0.6882 | 0.2475 | 0.0267 | 54.45% | 0 |
| platt_key | 0.6885 | 0.2477 | **0.0226** | 54.41% | 15 |
| isotonic | 0.6981 | 0.2498 | 0.0348 | 54.15% | 153 |

The pre-registered rule required **both** lower log loss **and** ECE no worse
than raw:
- Platt and Platt-key reduced ECE, but their log loss was worse.
- Isotonic was worse on both.
- Per season, Platt beat raw in 4 of 12 seasons and Platt-key in 3 of 12.

So the probabilities stay raw, and the pick rule is unchanged (raw argmax).

## What this means in practice

- **The direction is right; the exact number is rough.** Games the model
  leans on do cover more often. But a stated 60%+ has hit about 55%, and a
  stated sub-40% has hit about 40%. The extremes are **overconfident**, by
  roughly 4 to 10 points.
- **Treat the probability as a lean, not a number to bet on.** Anything
  between 0.45 and 0.55 is close to a coin flip.
- **The no-move games cluster.** There are no raw predictions in 0.48–0.50.
  The 845 games at 0.46–0.48 are mostly "market didn't move" games, where the
  estimator's key-number bias (Phase 4) puts P(home) near 0.476. Their actual
  hit rate was 0.489, close to a coin flip, and the pick is away, matching
  the pre-registered no-move fallback.
- **The key-number correction didn't hold up.** Platt-key improved ECE the
  most, but its key coefficient changed sign across seasons (−0.16 to +0.06).
  So the Phase 4 key-number bias was not reliably correctable out of sample
  with this data. It remains a disclosed limitation.

## Gate

The calibration report was produced: **PASS** (spec gate). The engine stays
uncalibrated by pre-registered rule, and the live slate says so.
