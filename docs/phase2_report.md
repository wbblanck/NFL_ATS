# Phase 2 Report — Null Model and Baselines

The full tables (per season and pooled, every source) are in
`docs/phase2_baselines.md` and `data/backtests/phase2_*.csv`. They are
regenerated by `python -m engine baselines`.

## What was measured

There are four fixed-side strategies, each graded against an opening line:
**always home, always away, always favorite, always underdog.** All games are
regular season only, and scores come from nflverse.

| Source | Role | Seasons | Graded games (W+L) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `league`: CBS Pick'em line recorded from the pool | **the real grading line** | 2026 | **0 so far.** Week 3 lines are recorded, but no game had a final score when this ran. |
| `sbro_open`: SBRO opening line | **primary historical proxy for the opener** | 2007–2021 | 3,722 |
| `nfl_close`: nflverse `spread_line` | closing-line proxy, **not an opener** | 1999–2026 wk 2 | 6,809 |
| `sbro_close`: SBRO closing line | reference | 2007–2021 | 3,740 |

SBRO ingestion covers all 3,856 regular-season games from 2007 to 2021, and
each one matched a unique nflverse game. 15 games have an unusable opening
cell and are excluded, not guessed: 11 negative spread cells and 4 where the
spread and total could not be told apart. One SBRO score disagrees with
nflverse (2016 KC@PIT), and nflverse scores are used throughout. SBRO's
closing line equals nflverse's exactly in 59% of games and is within 1 point
in 96%. The source book of either line is **UNVERIFIED**.

## Results (pooled)

**SBRO open, 2007–2021 (primary):**

| Strategy | W | L | P | N | Win rate | SE | z vs 50% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Always away | 1912 | 1810 | 119 | 3722 | **51.37%** | 0.82 pp | +1.67 |
| Always underdog | 1833 | 1762 | 118 | 3595 | 50.99% | 0.83 pp | +1.18 |
| Always favorite | 1762 | 1833 | 118 | 3595 | 49.01% | 0.83 pp | −1.18 |
| Always home | 1810 | 1912 | 119 | 3722 | 48.63% | 0.82 pp | −1.67 |

128 pick'em openers had no favorite and are excluded from the
favorite/underdog rows.

**nflverse close, 1999–2026 (reference):** away 51.04% (SE 0.61 pp, z +1.71),
underdog 51.10% (z +1.81), favorite 48.90%, home 48.96%. There are 190 pushes
on whole-number lines.

## Reading the results

1. **No baseline is distinguishable from 50%** at the usual 95% threshold,
   which needs |z| ≥ 1.96. The largest effects are away and underdog at
   about +1 pp, with z between 1.2 and 1.8. Each season is noisy: always
   away is above 50% in 11 of 15 SBRO seasons, but only one season (2019,
   57.9%) is individually beyond 2 SE.
2. **Pushes.** 119 of 3,841 SBRO-open games (3.1%) landed exactly on a
   whole-number line, as did 190 of 6,999 (2.7%) on the nflverse close. The
   league's half-point lines make those games decided wins or losses. How
   they would split is unknown, so they are reported separately and never
   imputed.
3. **The proxies are not the league line.** Week 3 showed the league line
   differing from the CBS odds-page open in 7 of 16 games and from Thursday's
   market median in 9 of 16. Every number above describes a proxy line. The
   league-line section fills in as weeks are recorded and played.

## The bar for later phases

Choosing the best of four strategies after seeing the results slightly
inflates it. With that caveat, the bar every later model must clear on the
**same source and seasons** is:

- **SBRO open 2007–2021: 51.37% (always away), SE 0.82 pp.**
- **League line: to be computed** once enough 2026 weeks are recorded. With
  about 16 games a week, one season gives about 272 games and an SE near
  3 pp. The league line alone cannot separate a 52% model from 50% within
  one season.

A model "clears the bar" only if its out-of-sample rate beats the bar by
more than the combined standard error. That is pre-registered here, before
Phase 3 is run.
