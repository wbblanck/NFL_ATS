# Phase 7 Report — Full-System Walk-Forward

The rules were pre-registered in `docs/phase7_preregistration.md` (commit
`2ab5202`) before the run. The full tables are in `docs/phase7_results.md` and
`data/backtests/phase7_*.csv`. They are regenerated by
`python -m engine backtest`.

## System and method

- **System:** `phase4-shifted-empirical-argmax`, market-only.
- **Method:** walk-forward one season at a time over 2007–2021. Each season
  is trained only on earlier seasons, back to 1999.
- **Proxies:** SBRO open stands in for the frozen CBS line, and the nflverse
  close stands in for the market at lock.
- **Why these seasons:** 2007 is the first season with an opening-line
  proxy, and 2021 is the last.

## Honest limits

1. **Not independent.** These 2007–2021 games were also used to design
   Phases 3–6, as declared in the pre-registration.
2. **Proxy lines.**
   - SBRO's open is earlier than CBS's Tuesday-noon line, so the real drift,
     and with it the real edge, is likely **smaller**.
   - The close is 15 minutes later than lock.
   - How much smaller the edge is will only be known from 2026 data.
3. **No 2022–2025 test.** No verified frozen-line proxy exists for those
   seasons.

## The independent test: live 2026

`python -m engine grade` grades every final prediction against the league
line you record. It runs automatically after each lock run and writes
`data/predictions/live_record.md`. **No skill claim will be made before 256
graded games** (pre-registered). At 16 games a week, that is about 16 weeks.
