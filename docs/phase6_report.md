# Phase 6 Report — Residual Features

The rules were pre-registered in `docs/phase6_preregistration.md` (commit
`a7e3e0a`) before anything ran. The full tables are in
`docs/phase6_results.md` and `data/backtests/phase6_ablation.csv`. They are
regenerated by `python -m engine features`.

## Result: **no feature group survived; all three are removed**

Walk-forward over 2010–2021, 2,979 games. Each group was added to the
market-based probability and fitted only on earlier seasons.

| Model | Log loss | ATS (P > 0.5) | Picks changed vs raw | Kept? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| raw (market only) | **0.6875** | **54.45%** ± 0.91 pp | — | baseline |
| refit, no features | 0.6882 | 54.45% | 0 | comparator |
| + QB change | 0.6889 | 54.28% | 49 | **no** |
| + rest / bye / short week | 0.6918 | 54.31% | 104 | **no** |
| + opponent-adjusted EPA efficiency | 0.6882 | 54.45% | 86 | **no** |

Every group made out-of-sample log loss worse than both comparators, and none
improved the ATS win rate.

## The features are real; the market already has them

A sanity check guards against a bug posing as a "fail". Across 2007–2021:
- The efficiency rating correlates **0.79** with the market spread and
  **0.34** with the final margin, so it measures team strength.
- Its correlation with the part of the margin the market *missed*
  (margin + spread) is **0.02**.
- QB change (−0.02) and rest differential (0.01) are similar.

By lock time, the betting market has already priced these public football
facts. That is why the spec tests features only as residuals beyond drift.

## Not tested (as pre-registered)

- **Weather.** The only historical weather in nflverse was observed at the
  game, which would be look-ahead. Deferred until real forecast captures
  exist.
- **OL / pass-rush.** Too little history for a fair walk-forward test.
- **Probability-weighted QB status.** There are no historical point-in-time
  injury reports.

## What this means

The live model stays **market-only**: frozen league line vs market at lock,
through the Phase 4 margin distribution. That is the simplest model, and it
is also the best one that was proven out of sample. The edge comes from the
league line being frozen while the market moves, not from out-guessing the
market on football facts.

## Gate

Ablation report produced. Only surviving features are kept, and none
survived: **PASS** (spec gate).
