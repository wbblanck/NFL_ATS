# Line-Move Predictor, Selective Rules: Pre-registration

Chosen on 2007–2014 only (docs/selective_preregistration.md); committed before any 2015–2021 result for these
rules. Same ratings, open, grading and −110 break-even (52.38%) as docs/move_preregistration.md.

2007–2014 exploration (win rate at the open): A and B agree in direction and both |D| ≥ 2: 54.6% (n = 712);
B |D| ≥ 6: 56.0% (n = 184). Other cutoffs 50–53%, not monotone (e.g. B |D| in [2,3): 50.0%).

- **R1** bet D's side at the open when ratings A and B point the same way and both |D| ≥ 2.
- **R2** bet B's side at the open when |D_B| ≥ 6.

Confirmation 2015–2021. PASS (per rule) if n ≥ 30 and the week-clustered interval at 97.5% two-sided
(Bonferroni over 2) has its lower end above 52.38%.
