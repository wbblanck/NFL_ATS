# Phase 4 Report — Cover-Probability Engine

The evaluation rules were pre-registered in `docs/phase4_preregistration.md`
(commit `4516fc3`, 04:07 UTC) before anything ran. The full tables are in
`docs/phase4_results.md` and `data/backtests/phase4_*.csv`. They are
regenerated by `python -m engine coverprob`.

## Setup

- **Predicted margin** = −(market spread at lock).
- **P(home covers league line S)** = P(margin > −S | market spread), estimated
  four ways.
- **Walk-forward:** 2007–2021, one season at a time, trained only on earlier
  seasons.
- **Target:** covering the SBRO opening line, which stands in for the frozen
  league line. The market uses the nflverse close.
- **Scored games:** 3,722. Excluded: 119 pushes on a whole-number open and 15
  games with no usable open.

## Results (pooled, out of sample)

| Estimator | Log loss | Brier | ATS of "higher-probability side" |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shifted empirical** | **0.6862** | 0.2466 | 54.89% ± 0.82 pp |
| Normal (σ fit) | 0.6863 | 0.2466 | 54.89% ± 0.82 pp |
| Kernel, mirrored | 0.6885 | 0.2478 | 53.73% |
| Kernel-local (keeps key numbers) | 0.6894 | 0.2482 | 53.17% |
| Coin flip (0.5) | 0.6931 | 0.2500 | — |

The D0-full drift rule on the same games scores 54.89%, the same as the
argmax rule.

## Decisions (applied as pre-registered)

1. **Default estimator: shifted empirical.** It had the lowest log loss. Its
   margin over Normal is negligible: 0.00006.
2. **Live pick rule: "higher-probability side."** The pre-registered switch
   condition, argmax ATS ≥ D0-full ATS, is met because the two are equal. In
   practice the two rules pick the same side almost always. The switch adds
   probabilities, not a different strategy.
3. **Gate: PASS (technical).**
   - P(home) + P(away) = 1 for every half-point line (tested).
   - Whole-number lines raise `InvalidLeagueLine`.
   - A sensitivity table (league line ±3, half-point steps) is written per
     game to `data/predictions/sensitivity_<season>_wNN.md`.

## Honest findings

- **The probabilities are weak but real.** Log loss improves on a coin flip by
  only about 1% (0.6862 vs 0.6931). That fits an edge of a few percentage
  points per game, not certainty. 90% of out-of-sample probabilities fall
  between 0.40 and 0.60; the 5th–95th percentiles are 0.39–0.58, and the
  median P(home) is 0.475.
- **The key-number-preserving kernel lost out of sample.** The local estimates were
  too noisy and sometimes extreme (probabilities down to the 0.0001 clip). It beat the shifted estimator in only 4 of 15 seasons.
- **Known bias in the chosen estimator.** It shifts every game's residual by
  its own spread, so it mixes integer and half-integer spreads and smears
  key-number mass:
  - Market exactly −3 vs league line −2.5: it gives P(home) = 0.503. The true
    value should be several points higher, because a 3-point home win covers.
  - Market −2.5 vs league −2.5 (no drift): it gives P(home) = 0.476, where
    about 0.50 is expected.

  **Probabilities near 3 and 7 are therefore biased.** The *direction* of the
  pick is usually unaffected. I am **not** swapping in a fix chosen after
  seeing these results. Phase 5 calibration, fitted walk-forward, is the
  pre-planned correction. A lattice-aware version (shifting only among spreads
  with the same fractional part) is a candidate to pre-register and test on
  2026 data.
- **Minor:** the live fit uses 2000–2026 (6,752 games). 1999 games have no
  kickoff time in nflverse and are dropped by the "kickoff before now" filter.

## Live effect

`lock-run` now writes for every pick:
- `p_home_cover` and `p_away_cover` (not yet calibrated),
- `model_margin` (expected / median),
- `model_version = phase4-shifted-empirical-argmax`,
- a `training_period` naming exactly which games the distribution used. Only
  games that kicked off before the run are used, and a test covers this.
