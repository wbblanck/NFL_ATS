How to read this page

Pool line and spreads. Each team shows its spread on the pool line. A minus sign means that team is favored. The market track underneath shows where the betting market stood at lock compared with the pool line, in the home team's spread: left means the market moved toward the home team, right toward the away team.

The pick is the side the market moved toward, with a cover chance from how games have finished relative to the closing spread since 1999. Provisional picks use the latest market snapshot and can change. The official pick is locked about 20 minutes before kickoff and never changes.

Historical win rates are 2007–2021. The 2025 holdout was weaker, at 52% on 1+ point moves. No claim of skill is made before 256 graded games.

Projections are tracked for comparison and do not make the pick. The player projection (proj-players-v3) adjusts team scoring strength for the starting quarterback, injured starters weighted by snaps, rest, travel and roof. Against the market it was a coin flip in 2022–2025 testing (docs/players_v3_report.md). "Injury report pending" means the game statuses were not yet published, so injuries are not reflected. It rates a listed backup quarterback from the depth chart and does not know if that backup is also out.

Hybrid (tracked) keeps the pick when the market moved 1+ point off the pool line and uses the team-only projection when it moved ½ point or not at all. It is followed for comparison only and never makes the pick (docs/hybrid_tracking.md).
