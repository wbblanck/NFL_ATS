How to read this page

Pool line and spreads. Each team shows its spread on the pool line, the number the pool grades on. A minus sign means that team is favored. "Market" is where the betting market stood at pick time, in the home team's spread.

The pick is the side the market moved toward, with a cover chance from how games have finished relative to the closing spread since 1999. Provisional picks use the latest market snapshot and can change. The official pick is locked about 20 minutes before kickoff and never changes. If no market line is available at pick time, the pick falls back to the away team.

The market line is Pinnacle's when the odds feed has it. Otherwise it comes from a backup source or the median of all books, and a pick made on fewer than five books is flagged.

Historical win rates are 2007–2021. The 2025 holdout was weaker, at 52% on 1+ point moves. No claim of skill is made before 256 graded games.

Projections are tracked for comparison and do not make the pick. The player projection adjusts team scoring strength for the starting quarterback, injured starters weighted by snaps, rest, travel and roof. Against the market it was a coin flip in 2022–2025 testing. When injury statuses were not yet published, injuries are not reflected and the pick is not counted in the record.

Hybrid (tracked) keeps the pick when the market moved 1+ point off the pool line and uses the team-only projection when it moved ½ point or not at all. It is followed for comparison only and never makes the pick.

The season path counts covers minus misses. Its first two weeks are backtest replays; the chips quote live games only.

A minus sign next to a team means that team is favored. Record graded against the CBS pool line. Projection models are tracked for comparison only.
